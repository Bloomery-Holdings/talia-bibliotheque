📲 Mettre l'app sur l'écran d'accueil · Put the app on the home screen
Chrome (round red-yellow-green ball): tap ⋮ at the top right of THIS page → “Add to Home screen” or “Install app” → Add.
Samsung Internet (purple planet): tap ☰ at the bottom right → “Add page to” → “Home screen” → Add.
iPad (Safari): tap the Share square-with-arrow at the top → scroll down → “Add to Home Screen” → Add.
If none of these menus exist, this page is open inside another app (WhatsApp, claude.ai…) — open bloomery-holdings.github.io/talia-bibliotheque in the real browser first. And even without the icon, everything works right here in the browser.
