What is still wrong — said plainly

None of this stops you looking at the book today.

Most screens put somebody in the room that the picture does not show. Read on 2026-09-08: 60 of 74 screens. Talia is missing from 52 of them, Mommy from 31, Papa from 19.

There is no drawing of a friend, a teacher, or any other child anywhere in this project. The whole school part of the day is an empty classroom and an empty playground. That is not four or five pictures — it is a hole nothing we own can fill.

The mountain, the lake, the train and the night window are coded shapes, not drawings. You said a coded triangle is not a mountain to a six-year-old. They are still coded shapes. Changing them is free — a downloaded cartoon costs nothing — and it is waiting on you.
